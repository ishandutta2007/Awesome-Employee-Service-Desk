## Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global IT and Employee Service Desk / Enterprise Service Management (ESM) market is estimated at approximately **$13.5B – $15.7B in 2025–2026**, on pace to exceed **$21B – $36B by 2030** with an annual growth rate (CAGR) of 12% to 15%. The market is **moderately fragmented**: mega-cap enterprise incumbents (like Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Atlassian) concentrate high-end enterprise workloads, while agile mid-market platforms (Freshservice, SolarWinds) and specialized AI-first autonomous support platforms (Moveworks, Leena AI, Espressive) capture significant, fast-growing niches without creating a single winner-take-all monopoly.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Frappe HR](https://github.com/frappe/hrms)**

  Complete open-source HRMS (GPL-3.0) with over 13 modules including employee management, onboarding, leaves, payroll, and **helpdesk ticketing** . Built on Frappe Framework with Vue-based UI. 8,883+ GitHub stars. Docker deployment with PostgreSQL. The helpdesk module enables internal ticketing for HR requests .



- **[Horilla HR](https://github.com/horilla/horilla-hr)**

  Free and open-source HR software (LGPL-2.1) built on Django. Features recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, payroll, performance, assets, and **helpdesk** in one application . PostgreSQL required. Docker Compose deployment. First boot takes 3-4 minutes for migrations .



- **[Znuny](https://github.com/znuny/znuny)**

  Fully customizable open-source help desk platform (fork of OTRS). Features customer interface for self-service, FAQ/knowledge base, ticket creation via portal/email/phone, SLA management, and 3rd-party system integration (CRM, catalogues, databases) . On-premise or cloud. Community-driven with 200K+ forum posts and 30K+ commits over 10+ years .



- **[iTop](https://github.com/Combodo/iTop)**

  IT Operations Portal — complete open-source ITIL-compliant service management tool (AGPL-3.0) . Features fully customizable CMDB, helpdesk system, document management, mass import tools, web services integration, service/contract management, change management, configurable SLA management, and graphical impact analysis .



- **[SafariDesk](https://github.com/safaridesk/safaridesk)**

  Modern open-source help desk (license varies) built for teams needing ticket operations, mailbox visibility, and SLA reporting . Django 5.0 + React 18 + PostgreSQL 16 + Redis 7. Docker Compose with 8 services . Features multi-source ticket creation (email, web, phone, chat, API, customer portal), SLA-aware operations, AI-powered knowledge base with pgvector semantic search, AI chatbot (Google Gemini), Slack/Teams OAuth integration, and Twilio SMS/WhatsApp .



- **[FreeScout](https://github.com/freescout-help-desk/freescout)**

  Super lightweight, self-hosted Help Scout and Zendesk alternative (AGPL-3.0) built with PHP/Laravel . No limitations on users, tickets, or mailboxes. 100% mobile-friendly. Multilingual (23+ languages). Seamless email integration with Microsoft Exchange authentication. Collision detection, push notifications, internal notes, and automatic refresh .



- **[Casilium](https://github.com/Casilium/casilium)**

  Self-hosted helpdesk and ticketing platform (Apache-2.0) built on Mezzio and Doctrine ORM . Multi-organisation workflows, SLA-driven due dates based on business hours, RBAC permissions with optional MFA, email-based ticket intake, and scheduled notifications. Modular architecture. Docker Compose deployment .



- **[Keptel TIMS (Trudesk fork)](https://github.com/sanjayy-gowdaa/tru-tims)**

  Enterprise Ticket & Issue Management System (based on Trudesk, Apache-2.0) designed for internal support across IT, HR, Facilities, and other business units . Centralized ticket management, multi-department support, advanced analytics and reporting, two-factor authentication (2FA), and real-time notifications. Docker Compose deployment on port 8118 .



- **[PostHog Conversations](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)**

  Support ticketing integrated directly into the PostHog platform . Multi-channel support via embedded web widget, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and email. Leverages PostHog's session recording and event data for context. Django REST Framework backend with Redis caching and PostgreSQL persistence . AI suggestion engine for drafting replies .



- **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)**

  Open-source IT Service Management software (GPL-3.0) with web-based interface . Features ITIL-compliant Helpdesk, automatic inventory, software deployment, Knowledge base, Problem/Incident/Request/Change/Release management, and Asset Management .



- **[Farik](https://github.com/SamoTech/Farik)**

  Modern open-source HRMS (MIT) for small and growing teams . Free features include People Directory, Recruitment Pipeline (Kanban), **Employee Helpdesk** for HR requests, Attendance & Leave, and Dashboard Analytics. Deploy on Vercel in 5 minutes or run locally with npm .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Frappe Helpdesk](https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk)** — Modern, streamlined open-source ticket management tool built on Frappe Framework. Dual portals for agents and customers, customizable SLAs, assignment rules, and knowledge base.

- **[Zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad)** — Open-source helpdesk (AGPL-3.0) with ticketing, live chat, telephony, knowledge base, and multi-channel support.

- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)** — Open-source customer engagement platform (MIT) with omnichannel inbox. Can be adapted for internal employee support.

- **[osTicket](https://github.com/osTicket/osTicket)** — Widely-used open-source ticketing system (GPL-2.0) with customer portal, knowledge base, and email piping.

- **[UVdesk](https://github.com/uvdesk/community-skeleton)** — Open-source helpdesk system (MIT) built on Symfony with customer portal and multi-channel support.

- Community **n8n** workflows for ticket automation and Slack alerts.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Frappe HR** or **Horilla HR** for integrated HR + helpdesk with **Znuny** or **iTop** for ITIL-compliant service management, **SafariDesk** for modern AI-powered support, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade employee service desk platform.
